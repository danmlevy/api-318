The Hiring Game

You are hiring a program director. Twenty candidates interview one at a time, in random order. After each interview you must hire or pass. Anyone you pass takes another job. You win only if you hire the single best candidate.

Interview scores use a new, unknown scale every round (one round might run 40 to 300, the next 610 to 1,450). So a score only tells you how this candidate compares with the people you have already met.

Play at least 3 rounds before you test a rule.
